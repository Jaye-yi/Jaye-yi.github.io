A path through making things

Researcher, builder, visual thinker.

I work on robot learning and embodied intelligence. I am interested in systems that identify what evidence they need, acquire it through interaction, and adapt when their assumptions fail.

From visual experiences to embodied systems

I began in Digital Media Technology, creating interfaces, animation, virtual worlds, and documentary work. Designing an experience meant considering both how it was implemented and how a person would encounter it.

Computer science gave me another set of tools for asking those questions. At Columbia I built visual manipulation pipelines and studied multi-agent interaction. At NRAO, I worked with incomplete observations in astronomical reconstruction. Explore the imaging research ↗ At Oracle, I learned to build and recover production systems.

These experiences now meet in robotics: an observation, an action, and a human response can all be evidence. I want robots to understand when that evidence can change their next decision.
